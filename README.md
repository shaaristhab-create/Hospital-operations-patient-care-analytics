# Hospital Operations & Patient Care analysis

 An interactive **Hospital Operations & Patient Care Dashboard** designed to provide a consolidated view of hospital performance, patient activity, financial metrics, and departmental KPIs.

 ## 📊 Dashboard Overview

 The dashboard helps hospital administrators and healthcare stakeholders monitor key operational metrics through intuitive visualizations and summary cards.

 ### Key Metrics

 - **Total Visits:** 6,500
- **Total Bill:** 10,21,40,877.67
- **Average Length of Stay (LOS):** 2.89 days
- **Average Patient Satisfaction:** 3.74

 ### 📈 Visualizations

 The dashboard includes:

 - **Monthly Patient Visits & Average LOS** – Tracks patient volume and average length of stay over time.
- **Monthly Patient Growth** – Shows monthly changes in patient growth.
- **Patient Visits by Department** – Compares patient volume across hospital departments.
- **Patient Satisfaction by Department** – Provides department-level satisfaction insights.
- **Average Length of Stay by Department** – Compares LOS across departments.
- **Average Waiting Time by Department** – Highlights differences in patient waiting times.
- **Total Billing by Department** – Displays department-wise billing contribution.
- **Total Bill by Insurance Type** – Breaks down hospital billing by insurance category.
- **Visit Type Analysis** – Visualizes the distribution of different visit types.

 ### 🏥 Departments Covered

 The dashboard provides departmental analysis for areas such as:

 - Pediatrics
- Orthopedics
- Neurology
- ICU
- General Medicine
- Emergency
- Cardiology
- Oncology

 ## 🎯 Purpose

 This project demonstrates how healthcare data can be transformed into an easy-to-understand dashboard for monitoring:

 - Patient volume and trends
- Department performance
- Patient satisfaction
- Waiting times
- Length of stay
- Hospital billing
- Insurance distribution
- Operational efficiency
